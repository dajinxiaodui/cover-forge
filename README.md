Cover Forge turns your photos and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art, in the browser, offline. Drop in your own photos (or try the two samples), place them in a hero panel plus two or three smaller panels, pick a colour grade — Sunset, Neon Night or Retro — write a bold title, and export a PNG in Square 1080 × 1080, Portrait 1080 × 1350 or Landscape 1920 × 1080. Drag a photo inside its panel to reposition it, scroll to zoom. Everything runs from local files; photos never leave your machine. All artwork is original — no Rockstar logos, art or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
